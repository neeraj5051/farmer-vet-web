# 📌 Project Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
**Humal** is India's digital livestock healthcare and breeding platform connecting livestock farmers in rural and semi-urban regions with licensed veterinarians.

The platform provides four primary services:
1. **Online Consultation**: Video/audio calls and diagnostic chat.
2. **In-Person Veterinary Visit**: On-site home/farm vet visits.
3. **Artificial Insemination (AI)**: Breed improvement services for cattle and buffaloes.
4. **Vaccination**: Timely preventive immunization schedules.

---

## 2. Product Purpose of the Admin Dashboard
The Admin Dashboard is an **enterprise SaaS analytics platform** (inspired by Stripe, Shopify, and Salesforce Lightning). It empowers founders, operations managers, and financial executives to answer 6 critical business questions within 30 seconds:
- How is the platform performing operationally today?
- How many bookings and live consultations are active?
- Where are operational bottlenecks occurring?
- How much total revenue has the platform generated?
- How is revenue distributed between Humal (commission), Veterinarians (payouts), and Statutory Taxes (GST)?
- Which districts, services, farmers, and veterinarians are driving growth?
